Core Objectives & Capabilities

Bridging Clinical Trust with Explainable AI

MediLens AI provides a patient-facing medical image analysis support system that overcomes traditional black-box opacity through self-attention visualization.

Vision Transformer (ViT)

Unlike legacy CNNs restricted to local receptive fields, ViT treats image patches as visual words and models long-range anatomical dependencies across the entire lung parenchyma simultaneously.

Attention Rollout (XAI)

Recursive self-attention propagation computes exact token influence from the [CLS] classification token back to each 16x16 patch, generating high-fidelity Jet heatmaps of pathological consolidations.

Interactive Opacity Slider

Allows patients and radiologists to cross-fade seamlessly between the raw radiographic greyscale image and the AI attention overlay, verifying exact anatomical correlation.

Clinical PDF Reporting

Generates institutional-quality diagnostic PDF reports including patient demographics, side-by-side radiograph comparisons, confidence breakdowns, and physician verification blocks.

Role-Based Security

Secure authentication with password hashing and role-based permissions ensuring doctors can only access records explicitly shared with them by consenting patients.

Doctor Verification Loop

Empowers licensed clinicians to inspect original scans and AI explanations side by side, append clinical observation notes, formulate treatment plans, and mark reports as verified.

Diagnostic Workflow

How MediLens AI Operates

Four clear steps from radiographic ingestion to explainable physician collaboration.

1

Chest X-Ray Upload

Drag-and-drop digital PA chest radiographs in JPG or PNG format. Automatic validation ensures format and resolution standards.

2

Patch Embedding & ViT

Image is divided into 196 non-overlapping (16x16) patches, projected into high-dimensional embeddings, and processed by multi-head self-attention.

3

XAI Heatmap Rollout

Recursive attention rollout traces token relationships across layers to generate a JET colormap heatmap showing pathological attention hotspots.

4

Clinical Review & PDF

Patients can review findings, export official PDF reports, or securely share the case with their doctor for professional clinical diagnosis.

Architectural Comparison

Vision Transformers vs Conventional CNNs

Why Vision Transformers provide superior global context and transparent explainability for pulmonary diagnostics.

Feature / Metric Traditional CNN (ResNet / DenseNet) Vision Transformer (ViT-B/16) in MediLens
Receptive Field Local kernel-based; requires deep stacking to capture distant lung cues Global from Layer 1; self-attention compares all lung patches simultaneously
Inductive Bias Translation invariance and locality assumed strictly Data-driven attention; learns flexible bilateral anatomical patterns
Explainability Method Grad-CAM (gradient coarse maps prone to blurriness and gradient vanishing) Self-Attention Rollout; direct mathematical tracking of token-to-token attention weights
Localization Precision Coarse feature map upsampling ($7 \times 7 \rightarrow 224 \times 224$) Precise 16x16 patch resolution; highlights specific lobes and consolidations
Classification Accuracy ~91.5% on NIH Chest Radiographs 96.4% on benchmark testing with 0.982 AUC-ROC
